Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

VeloFriend is built with a Privacy-First architecture. This policy explains how we handle your data, focusing on our commitment to local processing and user control.

Note on AI: VeloFriend uses "On-Device Machine Learning" to calculate your Rider DNA. Your raw activity data never leaves your phone for AI analysis purposes.

1. Visibility & The Velo Beacon

VeloFriend offers three levels of visibility to ensure you are always in control of your digital presence:

Private (Stealth)

Profile Not Public: You are entirely invisible to other users. You do not appear in discovery or search, and your location is never shared. The Beacon is disabled.

Public Profile

Social Presence: You are visible in "Discovery" so friends can find you. A fuzzed location (randomly offset) is shown on the map to indicate your general area without revealing your exact coordinates.

Beacon Active

Ready to Ride: Your real-time location is shared on the map for up to 1 hour to help others find you for a meetup. This requires a Public Profile.

Location Privacy Controls:

  • Area Fuzzing: Even when the beacon is active, you can choose to add a random offset to your marker in settings, showing a general area instead of your exact coordinate.
  • Privacy Zones: You can define "No-Ride Zones" (like home or work) where location sharing will automatically disable for your protection.

2. Health Data & Athlete Type

We access fitness data via Google Health Connect or manual input to fuel your Velo-Gotchi and calculate your Rider DNA.

  • Local AI Analysis: Our algorithms process your fitness patterns locally on your device to determine your Athlete Archetype.
  • Storage: Only the resulting "Archetype" label is stored on our secure servers if you choose to display it. Your raw, second-by-second activity data is NOT uploaded.

3. Social Interactions

When you interact with the community:

  • Waves & Join Requests: Sending a "Wave" shares your nickname and fuzzed location with the recipient to facilitate the meetup via our secure chat.
  • Velo-Gotchi: Other users can see your Velo-Gotchi's evolution stage, but not your private training logs.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data footprint:

  • Revoke Access: You can disconnect Health Connect at any time via Android System Settings.
  • Go Stealth: Switching your profile to "Private" instantly removes you from all live discovery features.
  • Account Deletion: Deleting your account via the app settings permanently purges your profile, friendship links, and history from our systems.
